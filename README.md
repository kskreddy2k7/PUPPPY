## ✨ Meet Your Desktop Puppy

Your own little puppy that lives on your desktop.

It can:

🐾 Follow your cursor  
🎾 Play fetch  
🏠 Rest in its own home  
🍖 Eat  
💧 Drink  
😴 Sleep  
❤️ React to you  
😊 Express different moods  
🔊 Make cute puppy sounds  
☀️ Greet you according to the time  
🌙 Remind you to take a break  
🎨 Be customized to your style  

The experience is designed to feel less like software and more like having a tiny companion on your screen.
